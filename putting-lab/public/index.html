Set impact timestamp; mark putter path samples and face line at impact. Ball speed requires a calibrated ball width, ball near impact and at least 3 post-impact timed ball positions. Camera projection, perspective and resolution limit accuracy. Distance of a putt cannot reliably be inferred from ball speed alone.

Angle and movement readouts are image-plane estimates. Calibrate camera geometry and validate against dedicated hardware before treating them as true face-to-target angle, dynamic loft, or real-world stroke path.

Video data policy

Video review and measurements run locally in your browser. Browser speech recognition may use your browser provider’s online recognition service; typed logging stays local. If you select “Keep video,” a clip is saved to IndexedDB on this device. The browser may evict stored data when space is low.

Measurement grades

MEASURED

Timing intervals computed from video positions selected by the user; recorded outcomes and putt distances are self-reported.

ESTIMATED

2D putter trace, projected angles, experimental pattern matching, and rule-based coaching.

CALIBRATE / HARDWARE

True 3D path, face-to-target angle at impact, dynamic loft, strike location, skid/roll, ball speed and launch characteristics.

Camera permissions

The live camera needs HTTPS or localhost and browser permission. Camera recording uses MediaRecorder when supported. On iPhone, open the hosted app in Safari.
